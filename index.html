Frequently Asked Questions

How much do wall to wall carpets cost in Dubai?

Prices start from AED 35 per sqm and go up to about AED 450 per sqm for premium ranges. We give a clear quote after a free measurement.

How long does carpet installation take?

A bedroom takes a few hours to one day. A living room or majlis takes about half a day to one day. A full villa takes 1 to 5 days.

Are your carpets kid and pet friendly?

Yes. We offer stain resistant, non-skid carpets with low pile options.
